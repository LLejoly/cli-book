---
title: The Workshop at Dusk
---

The workshop is where the house runs itself.

The housekeeping book, `make`, lists every chore and what each one needs first.
The translator, `python`, reads orders written in his own language and carries
them out. The foreman, `xargs`, hands a long list of names to clerks one by one.
The household calendar, `crontab`, rings the same errand every morning at seven,
whether anyone is awake or not. And the travelling trunk, `docker run`, carries a
whole small study inside it, so a job can be done the same way in any house.
The errand book that comes inside a parcel, `npm run`, lists errands a stranger
wrote, so you read it first. The cook's tasting spoon, `pytest`, is dipped after
every change. And the phrase book, `node`, lets Hobbs give orders in yet another
tongue.

The gas was low when Hobbs slid the last slip of the day across the desk.
It was written in red ink, and he would not quite meet your eye.

"Only to tidy up," he said. "It will be quicker this way."
