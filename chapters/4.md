---
title: The Master Key
---

"It is only to install a small thing," Hobbs said, and slid the slip across:
`sudo apt install`. The head librarian's master key. With it, every lock in
the house opens, including the ones you forgot you had.

Mrs. Finch's note was underlined twice: *Lend the master key for one door,
for one errand, and watch the clerk while he holds it.*

You lent it. You watched. It went well. Before and after, you glanced at the
calling card on the tray, `whoami`, to see in whose name the clerks were working.

That afternoon the house was full of clerks: one copying, one sorting, one
fetching coal. `ps` showed you the duty roster. One of them had been stuck
on the same errand since lunch, ringing his little bell. `kill` sent him home.
From then on, every long errand came with an egg timer turned over beside it, `timeout`.

Before supper Hobbs pinned a note on the board for every clerk to read,
`export`, and pointed at the tall green book on the shelf. "That one," he said,
"is where the house remembers."
