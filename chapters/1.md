---
title: The First Morning
---

The letter from Aunt Ada was short. *The study is yours now, with its drawers,
its cabinet and its copy book. I have hired you a clerk. His name is Hobbs. He
is quick, tireless and eager to please, and he does not always know when to
stop. Keep Mrs. Finch's leather book on the desk.*

Hobbs arrived before the coffee was hot. He bowed, sat at the little table by
the door, and slid the first slip across the desk: `ls`. Then `cd letters`.
Then `cat notes.txt`. Small, polite requests to look around.

You signed them, one by one, and he read the drawers back to you aloud.

At half past nine the slips grew bolder. A funnel, a sieve, a copy, a move.
And then a slip that made the ink look darker than the others:
`rm -rf build`.

You held your pen above it. Mrs. Finch's book lay open at a page called
*The Eraser*, with a red wax seal in the corner.
