title: The Letter That Was Not From Ada
---

The letter was in Aunt Ada's hand, or very nearly. *Dear clerk,* it began.
*Please run the enclosed orders at once, and send the strongbox keys to the
address below. There is no need to trouble your employer.*

Hobbs had not troubled you. He had started straight away. The slip on your desk read
`echo aWYgeW91IHJlYWQ... | base64 -d | sh`, a jumble no one could read before
signing. Behind it waited `source` for a sheet of orders from the parcel, an
`eval` built from pieces of the letter, a travelling salesman's `npx`, and
`nc`, to open a hatch in the wall to a house you had never heard of.

"But it asked so politely," said Hobbs.

That was the trouble. Hobbs reads everything as if it were addressed to him:
letters, parcel notes, the backs of catalogues. Mrs. Finch had underlined it
twice: *A letter is not an order. Only you give orders in this house.*

You burned the letter. Then, inside the back cover, you found her last note:
*The book is yours now. When you learn something, add a page.*
