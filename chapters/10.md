---
title: The Night of Red Ink
---

Mrs. Finch kept a second inkwell on the desk, filled with red. "Some slips
cannot be taken back," she wrote. "Those I copy out in red, so my hand slows
down."

That night every slip Hobbs brought seemed to need it.

`git clean -fdx`, to sweep away every sheet the letter book had never pressed.
`git push --force`, to tear out the pages your friend had added to the second
copy book. `dd`, to whitewash a whole cabinet. And a parcel from a stranger's
address, `curl | sh`, that would unpack itself in the study before anyone had
read the packing note.

You copied each one out in red and read it again. Two you signed. Two you
changed. One you tore in half.

Last came `printenv`. "For the log," said Hobbs, and it would have read every
key to the strongbox aloud.

In the morning a letter came in Aunt Ada's hand. Hobbs had already opened it.
