---
title: Spring Cleaning
---

Mrs. Finch's spring cleaning was famous. She never threw anything away before
she had weighed it.

So Hobbs fetched the letter scale, `du`, and weighed every drawer. He measured
how much room was left in the cabinet with the jug, `df`. He sorted the slips
into order on the rack, `sort`, so the heaviest drawers came first.
Where a line repeated the one above it, he wrote a ditto mark, `uniq`. With
the paper cutter, `cut`, he trimmed each ledger to the one column he needed.

Some sheets were needed in two rooms at once. Rather than copy them, Hobbs left
forwarding cards, `ln -s`: *this sheet is kept in drawer X*. And when you asked
who exactly would answer a slip called `python`, he opened the house directory,
`which`, and pointed to the name.

The house felt lighter. You could see the floor of the workshop again, and
in it, things you had never noticed: a calendar, a trunk, and a foreman's whistle.
